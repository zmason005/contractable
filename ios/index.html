Guess the daily Braille word in six tries. Enter a 5-cell Braille word, then use the feedback from each guess to solve the puzzle. You can type letters or braille.

Each row on the gameboard shows three groups:

  • ⠉⠕⠗⠗⠑⠉⠞ correct: dots that are in the answer
  • ⠛⠥⠑⠎⠎ guess: your guess
  • ⠺⠗⠰⠛ wrong: dots that are not in the answer

The correct and wrong groups build up across guesses. Your guess does not.

Think in Braille dots, not letters.

Valid word types

  • Common nouns: anchor, garlic, pillow
  • Base-form verbs: adopt, solve, switch
  • Adjectives: angry, silent, sticky
  • Words ending in -ed: learned, trained
  • Words ending in -ing: running, writing
  • Comparatives and superlatives: closer, biggest
  • Adverbs ending in -ly: slowly, rarely
  • Words for a person who does something: player, actor

Invalid word types

  • Plurals ending in -s or -es
  • Verbs ending in -s: allows, belongs
  • Proper nouns:
    • Places: boston, texas, tokyo
    • Surnames and first names: anderson, wagner, hoover
    • Days: monday, friday, sunday
    • Brand-like words: jaguar, casper
  • Nationality, language, ethnic and religious labels: german, jewish, asian
  • Profanity and mild expletives
  • Violence, crime and hate terms
  • Names of medications: xanax, paxil, insulin

Contractable: A Daily Word Puzzle for Contracted Braille

Contractable is a free daily word game built for braille readers, inspired by Wordle but designed around Grade 2 Unified English Braille (UEB) — the contracted braille system used by fluent braille readers worldwide. Instead of guessing individual letters, players guess five-cell braille words using standard UEB contractions, the same shorthand symbols found in braille textbooks, signage, and everyday braille reading materials.

Each day brings a new contracted braille word to solve. Contractable works directly with refreshable braille displays and screen readers like VoiceOver, making it one of the few word games designed accessibility-first rather than retrofitted for accessibility afterward. Every interaction — from entering guesses to reading results — is built to work naturally for braille display users and screen reader users alike.

The game is ideal for braille learners practicing Grade 2 contractions, teachers of the visually impaired (TVIs) looking for classroom warm-up activities, transcribers maintaining contraction fluency, and sighted developers curious how contracted braille compares to print English. Six guesses, one word a day, and immediate feedback on which contractions and cells are correct.

Whether you're a longtime braille reader or just discovering how contracted braille works, Contractable offers a quick, engaging way to build contraction recognition skills one puzzle at a time.
